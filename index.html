FAQ

Frequently asked questions about house painting in Houston.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Houston?
It depends on square footage, surface condition, and how much prep is needed. Interior rooms and full exterior repaints price very differently. We give free, itemized quotes after seeing the space — no guessing over the phone, and no surprise add-ons once we start.
How long does an interior or exterior paint job take?
Most interior rooms take one to two days. A full exterior repaint usually runs three to five days depending on the size of the home and the weather. We'll give you a realistic timeline with your quote and tell you right away if anything changes.
Do I need to move my furniture before you paint?
No. We move furniture to the center of the room, cover it, and protect your floors before any paint comes out. If you have anything fragile or valuable you'd rather handle yourself, just let us know ahead of time.
What time of year is best for exterior painting in Houston?
Fall and spring are ideal — moderate temperatures and lower humidity help the paint cure properly. Summer exteriors are doable if we work around the afternoon heat and rain. We avoid painting over damp surfaces, which is what causes peeling down the road.
What kind of paint do you use?
Quality exterior-grade and interior-grade paints suited to the surface and the Houston climate. If you have a brand or a specific color already picked out, we'll use it. If not, we'll walk you through options that hold up well here.
Do you paint kitchen cabinets?
Yes. Cabinet painting is part of our interior work — we clean, sand, prime, and finish so the result is smooth and durable, not tacky or streaky. It's one of the highest-impact changes you can make to a kitchen without a full remodel.
How do I get a quote?
Email or call us with a short description of your project — interior, exterior, or both — and roughly how much space is involved. We'll set up a time to look at it and get you a written quote at no cost.
